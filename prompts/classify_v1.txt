Choose the single best intent using these definitions:

- account_access: user membership, roles, permissions, adding or removing team members.
- rate_limit: requests rejected for rate limits (HTTP 429) or differing endpoint limits.
- quota_or_overage: usage quotas, overage, or spend limits being reached.
- billing_query: invoices, charges, plans, or payment questions.
- webhook_issue: webhook delivery, retries, duplicates, or signature verification.
- integration_help: connecting to or forwarding data to external systems, CI pipelines, or third-party tools.
- configuration_help: environment settings, configuration values, or overrides.
- data_export: exporting or downloading the customer's data.
- data_residency: where data is stored or processed by region.
- database_issue: database connection, restore, replication, or performance problems.
- performance_degradation: slow responses or higher latency in the customer's service.
- security_incident: suspected breach, unauthorised access, or active attack.
- compliance_request: audit logs, retention, certifications, or evidence for auditors.
- feature_request: asking for a new capability or asking about the roadmap.
- onboarding: getting started with the product.

Confidence guidance:

- 0.7 to 0.85 when the ticket fits one intent but another is plausible.

- Do not use 0.8 as a default; reflect how certain you actually are.
